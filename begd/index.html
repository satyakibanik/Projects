    About BEGD — Bangladesh Electric Grid Dashboard

    Explore Bangladesh electricity demand, generation, reported load shedding, fuel mix, power plants, regional supply and cross-border imports. BEGD brings together daily operating reports from Power Grid Bangladesh PLC and the National Load Dispatch Center (NLDC), recent trends, archived report comparisons and separate ERP real-time readings.

    All operating dates and times use Bangladesh time (Asia/Dhaka). See the sources and calculation details below for coverage, definitions and reporting limitations.

    Sources & how to read this dashboard

    Daily workbooks: PGCB daily operation reports.

    Overview, power plants, regions, imports, substations, operations, forecast and report comparisons use the selected Power Grid Bangladesh PLC / NLDC daily workbook. The header date picker selects exact archived operating dates with the supported workbook layout. Optional Yesterday’s data and Tomorrow’s data controls load adjacent operating-day workbooks for comparison. Real time is a separate view of ERP rows dated today in Bangladesh time from the demand/supply/load shedding, generation, and India import pages. Only ERP pages with rows for today are displayed; pages without matching rows are omitted. Real time refreshes every three minutes. Highest generation day reads the date shown on Power Grid's maximum-generation page, then loads that operating day's workbook. The workbook operating date can differ from its publication date. Choose an operating date in the header and select Load report, or press Enter. The same picker supports recent and older archived reports. The arrow buttons move between available operating dates in the published archive.

    All operating times use Bangladesh time (Asia/Dhaka). Generation totals include imports. Power is shown in MW; daily energy is shown in MWh or GWh. Daily energy uses published workbook totals, not sums of chart readings. Generation-side demand, substation-side demand, and reported load shedding are distinct measures. Regional figures are evening-peak snapshots, not full-day demand. Zero output alone does not establish an outage.

    Workbook references: P1 (summary, fuel mix, regions, shutdowns and event log), P2 (plant details), GenLog (24-hour unit output and import readings), P3 (substation load), P4 (demand and generation), En-Curve (half-hourly generation by source and shortage), EWIC (east–west flows), Voltage (substation voltage), and Forecast (day and evening generation availability). Forecast availability remains available in the Forecast tab; the demand and generation plot uses P4 actuals, L-Curve East/West generation, and the explicitly dated PGCB hourly Forecast Scenario. The first realtime chart shows substation demand, supply and reported shedding. Real time shows today's rank-one BEGD demand and generation forecasts. The Forecast view contains the complete BEGD comparison with operating-date and model selectors, dated PGCB scenarios, and matched-hour MAE scores. PGCB demand is scored against generation-side actuals, while BEGD demand uses substation-side actuals. Historical rankings and comparisons are retrospective. Substation supply is excluded from the forecast chart; positive load shedding is substation demand minus supply. Plant comparisons include yesterday, tomorrow, and highest-generation reports when selected above. Import route labels follow the workbook. Live sources: demand / supply / load shedding, generation by source, and India imports.
